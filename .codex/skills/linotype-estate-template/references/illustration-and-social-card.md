# Fountain Press illustration, first-page fallback and social-image contract

**Canonical skill procedure, 6 November 2026.** Maintained by `linotype-estate-template` for every *selected* book, essay or Governance chapter; the illustrated work's Git source owner retains rights, prose, figures, chosen principal image and canonical public social metadata. Fountain Press is the existing source-bound compositor and the sole authoring-time book/social derivative composer. EstatePublisher is the release and rollback authority. This does not create a second Reader, a separate image generator or an extra public publishing service.

## Domain-specific exception: Governance is text-first

**Writer correction, 6 November 2026.** The general illustrated-publication procedure below does **not** create an image requirement for `governance.fountain.coach`. Governance's completeness, authority and publication acceptance depend on full textual/semantic coverage, stable addresses, current/reference classification, source revision, rights, accessibility and independent readback—not on artwork, page heroes or per-chapter social cards. A fully text-only Governance chapter is complete. Do not fabricate artwork, enforce a 1200×630 card, or block a Governance release because the chapter lacks an illustration. Previously authored figures are optional non-normative source references and must not be mass-deleted. For ordinary Governance sharing, an already accepted neutral/typographic identity is sufficient; a first landscape Linotype folio is an optional, separately admitted social derivative. Do not publish the private v164 artwork inventory as a public mandate or retain the old 89-card backlog as a required task. The source-owned [Governance editorial policy](https://github.com/Fountain-Coach/governance.fountain.coach/blob/main/docs/GOVERNANCE-EDITORIAL-POLICY.md) binds this exception after canonical source admission.

Keep the following hero and social-card derivation procedure for **other source-authored illustrated works**, and for a Governance image only when the writer has deliberately accepted that specific optional supporting artifact. Preserve original image provenance and source relationships where applicable.

## 1. Source-authored images and fixed identities

At source intake, record **for every authored figure**: work/edition ID; ordered figure ID; original source owner and revision; local original asset path and SHA-256; role `hero` (principal) or `inline` (secondary); source paragraph/block address; intrinsic dimensions/ratio; alt text; caption if authored; author/credit, rights and licence. The principal illustration is the **first admitted hero in the text flow**; it does not become a speculative preceding full-page cover or migrate to an even-distribution point. If the source image occurs after a paragraph or heading, preserve that authored order and use the existing measured `inFlowFigure` with `data-inline-after-paragraph` from the *actual original* text position; `data-source-illustration-id` must be unique. Do not create artwork to satisfy a quota: text-only chapters are legitimate.

In the complete Governance v163 WLAN witness, source-anchored Figure 159 moved from visible folio **4/8 to 1/8**, with 160/160 chapter compositions and all **70/70** source figures on actual measured leaves, 67 first-authorized heroes and three secondary images. The unchanged original image URLs returned valid bytes; earlier tests counted hidden templates, which did not establish what the reader saw. This is accepted **placement**, not general social-image acceptance. See [v163 owning Estate receipt](https://github.com/Fountain-Coach/estate/blob/codex/press-sound-proof-gate-20261106/docs/acceptance/2026-11-06-governance-source-anchored-illustrations-v163.md).

## 2. One hero, one social visual, or one typographic fallback

For an eligible work with an admitted **hero**, its social card MUST be a **raster derivative of the very same source-owned illustration**, not a second invented scene or a generic estate banner. Produce one crawler-compatible JPEG/PNG asset at a declared share preset, preferably **1200 × 630** (approx. 1.91:1). Preserve original art, intended framing and aspect ratio: use `contain` with consistent source-owned background or explicitly authorized crop; never distort or remove attribution. Record original art SHA and raster derivative SHA, dimensions, compositing recipe, credited source and rights. An SVG in the article can remain SVG; a crawler card should use the verified raster derivative.

If **no hero** is admitted, the social card is **the publication's own first Linotype page composed in landscape**, not an unrelated default logo, a fabricated painting, or the first page of another book. Render from the existing Press compositor at the pinned source and exact immutable reader/CSS revision, using a documented landscape viewport and typography, without Chrome/navigation controls, private media or stage-only text. Extract the actual composed folio (or eligible facing-page spread according to the chosen landscape presentation) into a deterministic share-sized JPEG/PNG artifact and record input/source/renderer/CSS/fonts, measured page identity, dimensions and output SHA. Never rasterize every article as its normal Reader: this is strictly a *share derivative*, while the human pages remain semantic and reflowable.

**No source image and no accepted first-folio render is a failed social-publication gate**, not permission to re-use a generic estate image while claiming the fallback rule was met. Do not backfill historically published Governance cards without the Governance source owner's review and a separate release; distinguish candidate, staged and public image states.

**Book-level direct links and first-chapter social images (11 November 2026).** A Fountain Press Bookshelf book is also one canonical shareable document, separate from its individual chapter routes. Select the first eligible *illustrated* chapter in the currently admitted book/source order (not the first arbitrary asset in a directory), derive its own 1200x630 raster, and bind the first chapter ID, source image SHA/alt/credit/rights and output SHA to a static `/estate/books/<book-id>/` URL, initial HTML Open Graph/Twitter and Schema.org Book, plus a matching machine catalogue. The Reader opens the actual book contents in the same Linotype compositor. The Bookshelf must expose the route as a normal accessible link. The **first-image-per-book** requirement is distinct from chapter-level hero selection, never repositions a figure inside an authored chapter, and never fabricates art for text-only books. Rebuild and verify these static assets whenever a book's first eligible source image, book order, metadata or rights changes. See [direct-book link and sharing procedure](direct-book-links-social-image.md). This does not override the independently published image-free Governance exception.

## 3. Static source and crawler metadata

Publication source inventory binds `editionId`, `workId`, `sourceRevision`, `heroFigureId` (or no hero), `heroSourceSHA256` (when applicable), `shareImageSHA256`, MIME, pixels, alt, rights/credit, renderer/preset revision, original asset and **absolute public HTTPS card URL**. If no hero, bind exact first landscape page and page composition revision instead. A new source/figure/rights/font/pagination revision invalidates the derivative until rechecked.

Provide **one server-addressable static public work route** with the *approved* existing publication shell; crawlers must receive that work's canonical `og:url`, `og:title`, `og:image`, `og:image:width`, `og:image:height`, `og:image:alt`, `twitter:card=summary_large_image`, `twitter:image` **in the initial HTML without JavaScript**. The ordinary Fountain Press `#/read/<workId>` fragment cannot distinguish social images to crawlers: fragments are not sent in HTTP requests. Add a source-owned, immutable per-work sharing/static metadata route using the **existing Press publication project**, not a parallel new Reader or runtime HTML service. Keep the canonical Governance origin `governance.fountain.coach` authoritative for its chapter-specific public social cards; a Fountain Press derived edition may link to it but cannot silently republish source-owner metadata.

## 4. Verify and maintain as a release gate

Test a source chapter with hero **and** one without. Check original source figure and page composition (actual mounted leaves across iPad portrait, landscape spread and phone; source figure count, order, identity and no clipping). Separately verify derived social asset SHA, dimensions, MIME and rights/alt; no generic fallback, no unapproved crop, no source-private image. Fetch the **static per-work metadata page as a crawler** (no JS), verify its canonical URL and OG/Twitter fields, then fetch the exact image URL over the real staging host and independently read back its bytes. Stage art, page and metadata as one immutable source-locked Press artifact, with predecessor and rollback. For public promotion, repeat only after separately authorized EstatePublisher release; staging tests do not prove Facebook's own cache or published production. Any invalid/stale image, mismatched original revision, image loading error or missing metadata refuses *social* completion, but does not retroactively invalidate already independently accepted Reader text/navigation.

Every image edit, caption, source address, rights decision or page-layout revision triggers asset regeneration and the same acceptance. Preserve the previous release and exact source lineage; record a new terminal receipt rather than overwrite it. In Governance's audited source edition, all 160 chapter routes already had OG metadata, **89 referenced a generic governance-estate social image** and multiple others used SVG directly: that count is **historical audit evidence**, not a Governance acceptance gap after the writer's text-first correction. The former 161-card public candidate was uncommitted and retired rather than promoted; staging v164 remains only a prior release witness.
