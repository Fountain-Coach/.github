## Ranked targets: revise existing skills, do not create a duplicate

**Observed gap:** the existing canonical Skill is primarily a single short Realtime/Node audition procedure. The complete reading proved a *native Swift FCIS-KIT* long-form provider path with independent output verification and bounded automatic repair; do not falsely require the old Node audition procedure or substitute a retired model for a source-owned Swift instrument.

**Proposed procedure:** inspect current owner, official provider contract and existing instrument first; lock the complete manuscript revision and per-segment source/WAV hashes; keep source and original voice immutable; collect genuinely independent audio-to-text witnesses; classify strict matches, spelling-only variants, domain-name recognition ambiguity and actual lexical substitutions separately. Preserve negative receipts and candidate WAVs; tag `transcript-aligned` separately from `artistically accepted` and `released`.
