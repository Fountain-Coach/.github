## Apply the model, not its screenshot

1. **One source, one actual Reader.** Resolve the exact author-revision and currently admitted publication state. Reuse the production-derived Fountain Press compositor and existing book/collection navigation. Never create a parallel Reader, fixed canvas or image of a page to satisfy a layout test. The manuscript's original source addresses survive reflow and are not generated from folio numbers.
2. **Nested proof is real type.** A typographic setting inside a page is a live DOM type composition, not a static inset picture or another reader. The inner setting inherits the parent Satzspiegel and proportional margins as they change. Measure and paginate its actual source-character fragments in *remaining* folio space. Avoid both the 700px global leaf cap and the all-or-nothing specimen displacement seen before v150.
3. **Compose the two-leaf semantic field.** On a large screen, the spread is one reading relationship with two independently numbered folios. White space is not automatically defective: inspect source order and the next admissible semantic block. When a source-owned secondary illustration follows an argument but a fixed same-leaf prose reserve would push it entirely onto the next spread, measure if it can remain legibly in the current spread. A verso illustration may have its explanation on the facing recto; a recto illustration or single-page view retains room for a readable continuation on its own leaf. Choose the largest legible intrinsic-aspect image that fits; avoid thumbnails or forced equal-height columns. The v151 implementation searched down to 72% of preferred image width, **as an observed bound for that release**, not a universal arbitrary rule for every artwork.
4. **Bind heading to passage.** Keep an authored subsection heading with a measurable readable opening of the following paragraph, even after a secondary illustration. If the preceding image may shrink modestly without loss, measure rather than abandon the heading on the page foot. Do not move or delete source blocks to manufacture density.
5. **Mobile is an independent invariant.** On iPhone/narrow portrait and iPad portrait a single readable leaf remains the golden composition. Accept large-screen changes only when a before/after comparison proves compact pagination and source markup unchanged (or when an explicit separate mobile change is authorized). Recompute on resize and enlarged text; source identity and reader position persist.
6. **Semantic content precedes visual polish.** An essay enters with a concise source-grounded editorial abstract/proem, then its genuinely principal illustration where applicable. Later supporting illustrations are embedded *contextually* and keep source/intrinsic proportions, harmonized text before/after and useful captions. No invented social hero, no nested DAW or alternate transcript. The principal first illustration or a true first typeset page can supply share artwork, conditional on rights and public image availability. Keep theme, focus, Info/addendum, page controls and audio bound to the same book.
7. **Editorial proofreading is separate from runtime layout.** For *every active publication*, first establish the authoritative manuscript and the current publication/member inventory. Read the work and its abstract, proem, titles, credits, figure/caption, source notes, citations, and available audio. Check factual claims against its dated evidence and distinguish published/rehearsal/hypothetical abilities; remove interface explanations that obscure the work. Check spelling, grammar, punctuation, chronology, editorial voice, title/cover identity, continuity and accidental duplicate text. Then verify rendered page breaks, heading and caption attachment, source offsets, original figures, no overflow, mobile/landscape, dark/light, keyboard/focus and screen-reader labels. Log findings per work as **passed**, **corrected with a verified source edit**, **editorial decision needed**, or **not yet verified**; a mechanized typo/lint scan is not the same as actual editorial proofreading.
8. **Archive is a publication state, not deletion.** On explicit writer instruction, remove an archived work from the active edition's contents, gallery, chronological adjacency, selection options and current share inventory. Retain its exact authored text, source address, credits, original direct route and already published deep links; annotate the preserved Reader with an accessible distinct **archival-status editorial note** so a direct visitor does not mistake it for a current title. Document the date and source digest of the archival decision. Do not remove media/source evidence, silently repurpose the URL, or assert that staging archival changed public production. Existing Bookshelf/Store membership is a separate authority: don't claim persisted Store archival unless independently witnessed there.
9. **Exact comparative acceptance before staging.** Pin the original static Reader shell, the full source/asset manifest and previous immutable staging release. Recompose the *affected* source by the owning authoring path, compare before/after source-address sequence and image identities, and verify a photographed actual wide-screen failure against the candidate. Independently check compact-page parity and unrelated works. Retain rollback. Stage only the admitted changed static files under the existing SSH/release symlink guard; observe actual running HTTPS/WebKit bytes, not substituted preview assets. Keep publication production and hosted Skill installation separate.
