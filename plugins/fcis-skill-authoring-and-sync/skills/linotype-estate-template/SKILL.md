# Linotype Estate Template - FCIS-governed procedural projection

This skill explains how to conduct the work; it does NOT certify all FCIS chapters, create authority, define a new runtime capability, or supersede a typed Kit. Read [FCIS authority and reconciliation](references/fcis-authority-and-reconciliation.md) at the start of any implementation, publication, conformance, or architectural claim. The user's requested observable outcome fixes scope; the actual system fixes state.

## Fountain Press is the staging and production publishing standard

**Adopted by the writer, 6 November 2026.** For each admitted **book, essay, reading or Governance edition**, Fountain Press owns the **single source-bound publication composition** used in development review, canonical WLAN staging and subsequent authorized public production. Use the existing Fountain Press Linotype pages, Bookshelf, source addresses and pagination; do **not** build a separate stage Reader, a different production Reader, a truncated anthology, or a parallel CMS. The accepted book should not become a generic scrolling HTML article on either lane. The iPad portrait leaf is the reference composition, with facing leaves in eligible landscape; native iPad proof is distinct from desktop browser tests.

The source owner retains the authoritative manuscript or complete corpus, original route/edition identities, current/reference status, figures, links, rights and Git revision. Fountain Press projects those inputs into a pinned edition manifest, the adopted reader/catalogue and a reproducible, immutable publication artifact. **Reframe/FountainStore** owns writer admission and Bookshelf membership where applicable. **EstatePublisher** is the only authorized staging/public activation, delivery, rollback and receipt authority; Press cannot publish to DNS or impersonate that effect. For a non-book surface (e.g. Teatro Stage or a private Satzfunk player), preserve the source-owned experience while using the applicable adopted Press/publication-shell contract; do not invent a book or relocate its media rights.

**One artifact lineage across both environments:** review the production predecessor only to the extent required for the selected change, pin the owner SHAs and Press compositor revision, declare source/asset and stage-only host transformations, build an immutable candidate, and admit it to the **one canonical owner-WLAN staging** through the existing EstatePublisher delivery/guard. Verify all authored material, original anchors, media and reader behavior in the real stage; record full SHA inventories and a rollback-ready predecessor. Promote *that same reviewed composition and source identity* to public production only after a **separate** rights/publication decision and authorized EstatePublisher release, with independent public HTTPS/semantic/asset readback. If stage-only host differences require new bytes, record a deterministic, declared transform and its digests; never silently rebuild or swap the Reader between environments. Stage success does not confer public rights or claim production acceptance.

**Accepted example, not a universal required chapter count:** the complete Governance book reached immutable WLAN v162 on 6 November 2026 with 160 source chapters (72 current / 88 reference), 70 source-owned figures, 1,132/1,132 retained article links, and 160/160 live browser chapter checks, using the existing compositor. See the [source-pinned Press stage/production procedure](references/development-staging-production.md) and [Estate v162 acceptance](https://github.com/Fountain-Coach/estate/blob/codex/press-sound-proof-gate-20261106/docs/acceptance/2026-11-06-complete-governance-fountain-press-v162.md). This establishes **staging for that release**, not public production or physical iPad Safari/VoiceOver acceptance.

## Agent-readable publication maintenance — same edition, separate audience

**Required on a publication change that has an adopted or explicitly requested agent-readable surface.** The human Fountain Press Linotype book and its machine-facing representation are two projections of the **same exact authoritative edition**, not independently authored websites. Maintain edition/canonical identity, immutable source-owner revision, stable entry/source addresses, complete ordered contents, current/reference/draft distinctions, original links and figure/rights provenance, and declared public/staged status. An agent's interpretation must not depend on reading folio pixels or guessing the effective Governance chapter.

Before composing or releasing, read [agent-readable publication maintenance](references/agent-readable-publication.md). Inspect current owner-admitted metadata/JSON-LD/manifest and any existing machine routes before inventing a new format. When none is admitted, a minimal read-only machine-facing candidate may be authored and proven, but the example validator is a *fixture*, not a new mandatory platform API. Keep public, source-owned interpretation guidance as versioned **data** with a digest; never expose internal repository `AGENTS.md`, FCIS skills, host commands, secrets or private media as public instructions. Such guidance describes what is authoritative and permitted, but grants **no** agent execution authority.

**Parity is part of the publication acceptance:** compare the exact complete source inventory against human and machine projections (identities, source SHA, rights, statuses, links, media and version). Reject missing/demoted chapters, ungrounded capabilities or stale instruction digests. Exercise actual machine discovery/readback independently of Reader/browser tests on staging; repeat after a separately authorized EstatePublisher production release. Governance's current rules and historical reference chapters retain their *source-owned status*; they do not become instructions to a model merely by being indexed. `governance-publish` owns Governance source semantics; `fcis-skill-authoring-and-sync` owns agent Skill packages; `docs-sync` only projects derived documents; EstatePublisher remains the only release authority.

## Governance text-first exception — no mandatory illustrations

**Writer decision, 6 November 2026:** `governance.fountain.coach` publishes **normative text, stable semantic addresses, current/reference status, provenance, rights, accessibility and agent-readable interpretation**. Governance chapters are complete **without** illustrations, heroes, ornamental figures, image quotas or chapter-specific social cards. **Never** generate or require artwork to fill a Governance chapter; never fail the Governance Reader, machine-interpretation or EstatePublisher acceptance merely because an illustration or 1200×630 social derivative is absent. Do not turn this editorial choice into a new mandatory Governance chapter. Apply the source owner's [Governance editorial policy](https://github.com/Fountain-Coach/governance.fountain.coach/blob/main/docs/GOVERNANCE-EDITORIAL-POLICY.md) once admitted; keep the full canonical text/rules and their addresses. Existing authored figures and captions remain optional, non-normative source references; do not mass-delete them or promote them into authority. The older private v163/v164 placement/social-card witnesses are **historical staging evidence**, not an obligation to publish the 161-card candidate. A neutral typographic site preview is sufficient; an optional first Linotype page social image is a distinct approved distribution artifact, not Governance content. Do not silently publish older private card URLs.

**This exception is specific to Governance:** other illustrated books, essays and theatre works retain their source-authored hero/art and source-linked social-card requirements when those assets are part of the accepted work.

## Source-anchored illustration and social-image maintenance

When a publication includes an illustration, chapter hero, principal visual, **social media sharing image** or a **first-landscape-page fallback**, read [illustration and social-card contract](references/illustration-and-social-card.md) before composing/staging. Fountain Press must preserve the source owner's exact illustrated paragraph address: an image on a later authored paragraph stays there. The first *admitted principal* in-text figure is the hero; secondary art also remains at its original source beat. Use the existing Linotype figure anchoring rather than its equal-distribution fallback. Independently witness the artwork on actual **composed leaves**, not merely hidden source templates and HTTP 200 image assets. The accepted Governance v163 repair pinned 70/70 original figures on measured leaves, with the original Reader/compositor and original Governance source unchanged.

**Share policy:** If an approved hero exists, emit a crawler-compatible landscape **raster derivative of that same source art**, with aspect-safe `contain` or authorized crop, original/image SHA, credit/rights, alt and release identity; do not substitute unrelated new art. **Without a hero**, render the work's **first Linotype folio/spread in landscape** using the same pinned source/Press page composition into a prebuilt 1200×630 social asset; this is not a second Reader. Do not silently use the generic publisher logo/image in place of that fallback. Per-work static OpenGraph/Twitter metadata **must be served to crawlers without JavaScript**: a hash-fragment `#/read/<id>` is not a crawler-visible per-work URL. Verify actual stage/page and image asset bytes, dimensions and share metadata, and require separate source-owner/public EstatePublisher authority for production. The historical Governance v163 Reader proof established **figure placement only**. The writer subsequently removed illustration and per-chapter social-card obligations for the Governance domain: the previously noted 89 generic OG cards are **not** an outstanding mandatory Governance remediation.

## Start with a bounded decision

1. **Restate the concrete requested outcome and finite acceptance.** Do not change a viewing request into a deployment, a website fix into an architecture project, or Bookshelf admission into public release. New ambitions are separate follow-ups.
2. **Inspect the currently working path.** Identify the named Git source owner, branch and exact SHA, complete direct-static route, local preview, production HTTPS readback, canonical staging registry and *actual active stage symlink/service*. The registry's last observed release is historical when it disagrees with live state. Record last accepted evidence and failures as OBSERVED / PROVEN / DOCUMENTED / INFERRED / PROPOSED. Never replace live bytes with arbitrary latest `main`.
3. **Resolve applicable FCIS.** Read the organizational [Goal Fidelity and Non-Invention rule](https://github.com/Fountain-Coach/.github/blob/main/docs/FCIS-Non-Invention-and-Goal-Fidelity.md), applicable sections and amendments of [published Governance](https://governance.fountain.coach/), current repo instructions and typed Kit/Store/EstatePublisher authority. Record version/commit, publication status, conflicts and inaccessible sources. Never mistake a static reference or local draft for the accepted current rule. This is scoped source reconciliation, not an invented universal compliance service.
4. **Detect the Invented-Prerequisite Loop before declaring a blocker.** Apply the four-question FCIS test: original observable goal; actually failed operation; why the working mechanism is insufficient; smallest correction with same-path acceptance. The loop is *goal substitution -> speculative prerequisite -> implementation -> passing tests used to claim necessity -> new dependency -> moving Definition of Done*. If the new necessity is unproven, reclassify it as PROPOSED/NON-BLOCKING and continue on the existing proven path. Read [the named loop and EstatePublisher incident](references/invented-prerequisite-loop.md). Do not make the writer an operator or test harness.
5. **Select the owner boundary.** Reframe/FountainStore own author-approved Bookshelf membership; Fountain Press Linotype owns book rendering; Fountain Coach Publication Core governs complete, checked-in, direct-static routes; EstatePublisher owns governed previews/check-in and public promotion; deployed HTTPS/WebKit is a *read-only* observation authority. Read [development / staging / production](references/development-staging-production.md) whenever an estate environment or staging transition is in scope, [publication lanes](references/reframe-publication-lanes.md) for Bookshelf or release tasks, [template contract](references/template-contract.md) for shell/layout questions, and [bounded implementation guidance](references/implementation-prompt.md) only for a demonstrated defect.
6. **Keep the three authorities distinct.** **Development = Git** (source owners, exact commits, intended changes), not a DNS-based environment. Its browser view is the existing lease-bound native local preview. **Staging = the single registered owner-WLAN Ubuntu Mac mini** with an immutable release and active symlink. **Production = currently deployed public HTTPS bytes and separately retained publisher receipts.** Production observes published reality; it does not overwrite authored Git or grant editorial rights. Do not provision `dev.*`, a new DNS entry, a fourth service or an alternate publisher to represent development.
7. **Apply production-first lineage only to the requested canonical Git-to-staging transition.** See [development / staging / production](references/development-staging-production.md). The author explicitly wants the selected published production state to be the starting point for a development-derived staged candidate. Independently verify the *affected route/asset scope* using existing production release inventories and observed HTTPS bytes; reuse a valid immutable baseline rather than force a redundant import or a whole-estate crawl for a route-scoped change. Record exact Git owners/SHAs, intended delta, preserved content and WLAN-only transformations. Compose the candidate off-line, then check the production and staging predecessor again before using the already-established EstatePublisher SSH/rsync + SHA-256 + atomic symlink + readback path. Never activate bare production as a temporary public staging state or erase retained stage work. Staging inspection and local Git previews require no production-first ceremony.
8. **Separate the requested guarantee from ordinary publication, and preserve the established SSH contract.** Inspect the installed EstatePublisher implementation, not only an open PR. Its historical `previousReleaseID` checks the stage predecessor but does not alone prove the production baseline. When the writer explicitly requires a *guaranteed* production-first transition, do not certify that guarantee without observed native checks and correlated evidence; address a demonstrated missing check by the smallest change in the existing EstatePublisher client/contract. **Do not freeze an otherwise authorized, working static publication merely to install that proposed check**, unless the specific requested outcome depends on the guarantee. Existing authenticated OpenSSH, rsync, SHA-256, atomic symlink activation, rollback and independent readback are the accepted transport; do not require a new restricted account, signing ceremony, helper, SecretStore enrollment, daemon, GitHub CI, DNS zone or permission refactor absent the FCIS four-question proof. The skill cannot impersonate runtime enforcement or independent publication authority.
9. **Verify claims relevant to the goal.** Structural HTML helper: `python3 scripts/check_shell_snapshot.py --contract <accepted-contract.json> --site estate=<staged-html> ...`. It tests a *declared static fixture/schema*, not lineage, FCIS, CSS, WebKit, rights, deployability or production. For visual parity use existing EstatePublisher/WebKit mirror and native Safari/iPad evidence where necessary. For a **new** public route, first reuse the accepted domain template and applicable named preview/scenario acceptance; check inherited navigation, active state, Info, theme, footer, visual geometry and relationships before packaging, as described below. For a stage transition, keep production-source verification, candidate inventory, live stage receipt and browser/readback evidence separate.
10. **Publish publicly only under separate authorizing intent.** WLAN staging is not production. Production takes an already-built immutable digest-bound artifact through an authorized EstatePublisher plan, preserves rollback, and is confirmed with typed receipts and independent public readback. Never build on production, copy private Satzfunk media/tokens, or claim public publication from stage, Git push or HTTP 200 alone.
11. **Stop when the requested outcome is proved.** Report separately source state, baseline lineage, declared Git delta, stage predecessor/current release, native enforcement state, tests, browser witness, public release, and open uncertainties with links, SHAs, receipts and exact bounded failures. ## Reader-facing Linotype: the page is the frame

When the task changes a **reader, folio, page composition, semantic illustration, source-linked audio, or responsive book experience**, read [page-is-the-frame.md](references/page-is-the-frame.md) before implementation. For **SVG diagrams, framed illustrations, visible text inside a diagram, or annotated Teatro Scores**, also read [semantic illustration ink boundaries](references/semantic-illustration-ink-boundaries.md): align the outer figure to the *Satzspiegel* but **measure the text glyph bounds against each drawn internal rectangle**, not just the HTML image box; physical iPad portrait is the golden single-leaf reference, with separate corrected-device acceptance. When it involves Vergil, *Illustrating Inferno*, British-English narration, voice-over, or a narrated edition, also read [Vergil — narrator and voice specification](references/vergil-voice.md); keep the narrator persona independent of any provider voice ID or optional self-recorded voice. For **reader navigation, Bookshelf collection identity and coordinated text/gallery views, contents geometry, page transitions, or Teatro Scores**, also read [reader-navigation-and-score-composition.md](references/reader-navigation-and-score-composition.md) before design or implementation; the interactive organigram is writer-accepted design, not live acceptance. When attaching an audio edition to an evolving Bookshelf, read [new-estate spoken-edition binding](references/new-estate-spoken-edition.md) and verify which *actual current reader* owns the work before editing any standalone/public edition.

- **Compose into pages; do not squeeze content into a screen.** Keep readable typography and repaginate into additional leaves when content or accessibility settings need more space. Treat portrait as one leaf and show a spread only when both leaves remain readable.
- **Keep source identity stable and page identity disposable.** Reflow, rotation, viewport change and text enlargement may change page numbers; preserve the addressed passage and reading position.
- **Reduce reader burden.** Keep the work primary. Expose only controls needed for the present reading/listening action; move architecture, production status, provenance detail and authoring machinery behind appropriate secondary surfaces.
- **Test semantic frame padding independently of image padding.** A figure and caption may align correctly with the type area while drawn text exceeds its own internal border; use browser ink bounds (e.g. SVG `getBBox()`) for every line, compare both visible internal insets, and re-lineate only the figure's specimen without changing source words. Do not hide the mistake with cropping, whole-book scaling or new reader furniture.
- **Use semantic media only when it clarifies.** An illustration should remove explanatory burden, not add another object that needs interpretation. Treat listening as a property of the source passage/page, not a second application nested inside the reader. For British Vergil, the written page is the one transcript: read its exact admitted text, preserve page-integrated controls and never insert a second transcript or Continuous mode.
- **State the present.** Reader-facing prose and status must describe observed current capability. Do not announce hypothetical future tests, roadmap states or unperformed transfers as part of the work's present identity.
- **Keep rigor underneath.** Simplicity never weakens source ownership, accessibility, rights, evidence, publication or rollback contracts. Hide machinery from the reader only when the reader does not need it; do not delete the machinery or its proof.

## Specific publication and shell invariants

- **Book is not page.** A source revision, composed folio, Empty slot, score, player and public edition are distinct identities. An Empty becomes a book only after an attributed authorized writing/admission act. Reframe Bookshelf membership never implies DNS, a reel icon or public redistribution.
- **One publication authority, no generated web shell at runtime.** Chapter 127 describes a versioned Fountain Coach Publication Core embodied in the complete checked-in static HTML/CSS/JS of each route, not a new wrapper or generator. A measured Linotype reader is a source-bound book compositor; it is not the global site shell.
- **Shared estate semantics, independent authored bodies.** Preserve the source owner, masthead, approved editorial navigation, focus/active states, footer rights/provenance and responsive behavior. A pinned historic nine-item reel is only binding when the current adopted route/manifest proves it; unresolved public destinations must not be invented. Status remains a supporting/legal surface according to current policy.
- **Different kinds of proof remain different.** Identical link lists do not prove shared visual geometry; a screenshot does not prove source identity, Store admission, accessibility, media rights or public release.
- **Production-first is a lineage relationship, not two visible deployments.** Reuse a verified production baseline off-line; a candidate for the author-requested production-first stage is baseline plus explicit Git delta. A stage predecessor check proves only stage concurrency. Missing lineage proof means *guarantee not established*, not automatically "SSH publishing is broken" or "install new security machinery". See the [Invented-Prerequisite Loop](references/invented-prerequisite-loop.md).
