# FCIS intellectual property and publication boundary

**Status:** Owner-directed public disclosure and rights-preservation policy, 25 November 2026. Not a legal opinion, patent filing, new licence grant, rights assignment or assertion of registered marks.

## Ownership and purpose

Fountain Coach maintains its organization-authored FCIS technology, architecture and source contracts. The customer retains rights in their own data, conversations, work evidence and tenant-derived knowledge; no implicit transfer to the platform, model provider or public repository. Preserve each existing third-party and contribution licence, standards rights, and prior authorized public projection. Record actual rights-holder and contribution authority privately where needed; neither commit authorship nor a GitHub organization name is by itself chain-of-title proof. See [NOTICE.md](../NOTICE.md).

## Disclosure boundary

The organization root `Fountain-Coach/.github` is a public reference and interoperability surface. Its public visibility and existing forks must be treated as actual disclosure, including branches, PRs, issues, Actions logs, artifacts, release assets and full Git history. Publication does not create confidentiality. Keep only owner-approved publication material there: high-level product behavior, protocol schemas and non-sensitive conformance obligations; public FCIS-GO projection and historical already-published sources remain governed by their existing explicit policy. Do not place new private source or evidence in a public branch for review.

Use the existing private implementation repository and native tenant FountainStore for protected implementation, unpublished invention details, private Skill payloads and customer history/evidence. Use SecretStore for credentials. No new repository or identity system is implied. For publication, review and minimize exact changed bytes before push; maintain approved source/projection manifests and provenance, validate locally before public transfer, and retain signed reviewed revisions. The existing public Skill path guard is useful but not a content classifier or a guarantee against disclosure. Do not rely on post-push CI to undo publication. No blanket relicensing, automatic publishing, broad new licence, rights assignment or public/private visibility change without owner approval.

## Practical protection

- Separate public reference contracts from confidential implementation and commercial know-how. Apply least-privilege access, protected review/branch rules, strong account authentication, private work locations, and confidential handling. Preserve access/release/provenance records. Review the existing private source and licensing/permission inventory; do not publish that inventory's sensitive contents.
- Preserve actual dated authoring, reviewed commit/release identity and source digests as evidence. Verify contributor rights/permission before accepting external work; retain explicit licences and copyright notices for third-party components. A commit or copyright notice is evidence, not proof of all rights or a patent.
- Before disclosing a potentially patentable technical invention, seek appropriate patent counsel and record the disclosure decision privately. Public disclosure may defeat novelty; making a repository private later cannot undo prior public copies. Consider brand clearance and trademark registration separately; no current registration is assumed.
- Protect trade secrets with real confidentiality measures, not a label on public data. German law distinguishes copyright-protected expression from underlying ideas and interface principles, and trade-secret protection requires reasonable secrecy measures. No policy promises absolute exclusion of independent implementation or recovery of already published copies.
- Maintain customer/owner portability, authorized exports and deletion/retention policy independently of provider choice. No customer or private source enters public Git through learning, trace data, automated promotion, test fixtures or marketplace sync.

Official references: [GitHub repository licensing](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/licensing-a-repository), [UrhG §69a](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__69a.html), [GeschGehG §2](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/geschgehg/__2.html), [DPMA patent protection requirements](https://www.dpma.de/schuetzen/patente/patentschutz/schutzvoraussetzungen).

## Release discipline

Before publishing, identify the exact public contract scope, rights/provenance and protected exclusions; refuse private contents and unreviewed rights changes. Existing reviewed public distribution remains intact. Keep implementation-specific material in already authorized private custody; independently record owner review and source digests in the existing release process. No public file, policy or copyright notice is itself proof of legal entitlement, private-repository access controls or statutory compliance. Assess existing public history and rights separately; do not rewrite or delete history to pretend an earlier disclosure did not occur.
